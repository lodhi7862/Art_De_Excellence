Our Heritage & Innovation

Crafting Tomorrow's Heirlooms Today

KAMAIZ began with friendship, a shared respect for craft, and a belief that growing jewelry businesses deserve the right team beside them.

Built on Friendship. Guided by Craft.

Fourteen years ago, Kamil and Faizan entered jewelry retail at different companies, building separate careers. As independent retailers, wholesalers and emerging brands asked for help bringing ideas into production, one freelance project became many. The message was clear: there was a need for what they could do together.

From Bangkok, we coordinate design, development and production with a trusted network of specialist manufacturing partners. KAMAIZ is formed from our founders' names and stands for Perfection & Blessing: care in every detail, and gratitude for every client who trusts us.

Your design remains your intellectual property. Project files and specifications are shared only with the professionals directly involved in production.

How We Work

From First Sketch to Final Delivery

01. Concept & Technical CAD

Your sketches, references or ideas become precise 3D models, engineered for dimensions, stone placement, metal requirements and production feasibility.

02. 3D Resin Prototyping

A high-resolution resin prototype lets you inspect the design, check fit and estimate weight before production begins.

03. Precision Casting

Approved designs are cast by specialist partners in 18K gold, platinum, 925 silver or other specified alloys, with attention to metal quality and structural integrity.

04. Micro-Pavé & Gemstone Setting

Experienced setters work under professional microscopes to ensure precise alignment, secure stones and beautiful light performance.

05. Hand Finishing & Surface Refinement

Filing, polishing, texturing and plating are completed to your specification, with edges, surfaces and fine details reviewed throughout.

06. Quality Control & Final Delivery

Each piece is checked against the approved CAD design and specifications, including dimensions, weight, stone security and finish, before packaging and delivery.

Customer Support

FAQs: Jewelry Design & 3D Printing

Answers about the design process, files, pricing, delivery, measurements, printing, and support.

About the Design Process

How do you turn my idea into a CAD model?

I study your references, understand your style, confirm measurements, and then create a precise 3D CAD model using professional software. You receive previews at each stage.If you can’t visit a store or want to keep the ring a surprise, measure the finger’s size in millimeters. Then, use the chart to find the correct ring size.

Can I send sketches or reference images?

Yes. Any sketches, photos, or sample jewelry help me match your exact vision.

How many revisions are included?

1–2 moderate revisions are included to fine-tune details before final approval.

Can you create a matching full set?

Yes, I can design a complete coordinated set (ring, pendant, earrings, bangle) with matching style and proportions.

How do I determine my ring size?

If you do not know your size, our recommendation is to click on Ring size find out your size.

Files & Output

What files will I receive?

You get STL, 3DM, OBJ and high-quality rendered images for approval or marketing.

Are files production-ready?

Yes, models are made with correct wall thickness, prong sizes, tolerances, and casting requirements.

Do you provide realistic renders?

Yes. I create photorealistic images so you can clearly visualize the final piece.

Are the files suitable for printing/casting?

All models are optimized for smooth 3D printing, clean burnout, and perfect casting results.

Pricing & Quotation

How do you calculate the design cost?

Cost depends on design complexity, detailing, number of stones, and time required.

Do you charge separately for each piece?

Yes, each item (ring, earring, pendant) is priced individually because each design requires separate work.

Is printing cost separate?

Yes, printing involves different materials and time, so it is billed separately.

Do you offer package prices for bridal sets?

Yes, full bridal set packages come with discounted bundle pricing.

Delivery Time

Delivery Time

Normally within 24–48 hours depending on complexity.

Final production-ready file delivery time?

Usually 2–4 days after approval of the first draft.

Do you offer urgent service?

Yes, express and same-day modeling is available with an additional rush fee.

Measurements & Size Details

How do I determine my ring size?

If you do not know your size, our recommendation is to click on “Ring Size” to find out your size.

What measurements do you need?

Ring size, widths, stone sizes, wrist size for bangles, pendant dimensions, or any custom requirements.

Do you follow size charts?

Yes, US, UK, and Indian size systems are supported.

How do you ensure proper stone spacing?

I use accurate pavé/channel spacing rules and check structural strength during modeling.

Stone Setting & Gem Layout

Can you create a stone placement map?

Yes, I provide detailed stone layouts showing positions, count, and sizes.

What setting styles do you support?

All major settings: prong, micro-pavé, bead, channel, invisible, tension, etc.

Can you recommend stone sizes?

Yes, I guide you on ideal sizes for balance, strength, and aesthetic result.

Do you provide gemstone weight estimation?

Yes, I can estimate stone weight based on the model.

3D Printing & Master Model

What materials do you use?

High-quality castable resin designed specifically for jewelry manufacturing.

Are prints suitable for direct casting?

Yes, prints are fully compatible with investment casting for clean burnout.

Maximum print size?

Up to approximately 10 cm depending on the model and machine.

Do you offer casting services?

I provide printing only, but I can guide you on best practices for casting.

Design Modifications

Can you modify an existing design?

Yes, I can edit old CAD files, recreate missing files, or improve older models.

Can you adjust ring size, thickness, or stone size?

Absolutely — all dimensions can be customized as needed.

Cost for revisions after final approval?

Small changes have a minor fee; major redesigns require a new quote.

Production Support

Do you provide manufacturing guidance?

Yes, I offer support on thickness, prongs, weight control, and technical improvements.

Can you assist with stone setting issues?

Yes, I review spacing, prong angles, and overall gemstone layout.

Do you help with mold-making and finishing?

Yes, I provide guidance to ensure smooth mold-making and clean finishing.

Confidentiality

Will my design be kept confidential?

Yes, your designs are private and never shared.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes, NDA signing is available on request.

Do you reuse client designs?

No every design is exclusive to each customer.

Order Requirements

What information do you need to start?

Reference images, measurements, stone list, preferred style, and any special instructions.

Do you need high-resolution pictures?

Yes, clear images help create an accurate design.

What file formats can I send?

Images, videos, sketches, PDFs, or any format that clearly shows your idea.

Shipping & Delivery (Printing Only)

Do you ship printed models internationally?

Yes, international shipping is available.

Do you charge for shipping?

All international orders are subject to a shipping charge. International duties, taxes and fees are the full responsibility of the buyer and will be collected by the carrier prior to delivery.

How can I track my order?

Please refer back to your order confirmation email, which includes a link to track your order progress.

Can I reroute my package?

For insurance reasons, we are not able to reroute packages once they ship.

What do I do if the package I received looks tampered with?

If your delivery package appears to have been tampered with, please refuse delivery and contact us immediately via email at info@artdeexcelence.com

Where do you ship from?

All online orders are shipped from our Bangkok Thailand headquarters.

Do you ship internationally?

Art de excellence is proud to offer delivery to most international destinations. Please note the customer is responsible for any and all duties, taxes and fees applied to the shipment by local customs.

Will I be charged duties, taxes and other fees?

Please note the customer is responsible for any and all duties, taxes and fees applied to the shipment by local customs.

What packaging do you use?

Shock-resistant and moisture-protected packing to keep prints safe.

Which courier companies do you use?

DHL, FedEx, Leopards, or any courier you prefer.
